3. Founder-approved Workstream C consolidation approach. The proposal is preserved unchanged with its original proposal-era wording; the later approval is recorded in the current authority register and governs.

Prefer the strongest and most suitable evidence-backed direction; do not keep an old decision merely because it exists. Bring material product-level changes to the founder before adoption.

## 5. Non-negotiable working principles

- One project lead: Fable.
- Approximately 3–5 supporting models/contributors may be used, selected by Fable per task; no parallel leadership.
- Technical choices are delegated to Fable.
- Product purpose, goal, scope, professional promise, learner journey, target-user/market, and strategic direction remain subject to founder approval for material changes.
- All previous decisions can be challenged with evidence. Founder approval is not immunity from criticism; it is authority over final product direction.
- Every element must earn its place. Prefer useful value over feature count, novelty, complexity, AI usage, or competitor imitation.
- Simplicity for beginners is a product-wide UX requirement. Complexity belongs in the system, not in the learner's experience.
- KEEP / IMPROVE / RETHINK / REPLACE / COMBINE / REMOVE / VALIDATE are legitimate outcomes.
- Do not damage another product area when improving one. Map dependencies and end-to-end consequences.
- The first assignment is read-only. Implementation needs separate explicit founder authorization.
